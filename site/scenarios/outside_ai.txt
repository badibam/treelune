# With an outside AI: the external access opened in the app, then the Claude app on the same phone asked in one message.
story: Starts in the app, its external access opened. Switches to the Claude app and, in one message, asks for the week's runs and to note a run of 6 km today. Ends in the Claude app, the request done.
setup: Theme Cut paper, light. The app on Settings › External access, closed. The Claude app connected to the app's external access, on a new conversation, its conversations panel closed and nothing of the account on screen.
start
do: Open the external access
do: Switch to the Claude app, then touch its message field
type en: Use the Treelune connector: what were my runs this week? Then note a run of 6 km today in Runs.
type fr: Utilise le connecteur Treelune : quelles ont été mes courses cette semaine ? Puis note une course de 6 km aujourd'hui dans Runs.
do: Send, wait until the answer is complete, the run noted, and hold a second
stop
